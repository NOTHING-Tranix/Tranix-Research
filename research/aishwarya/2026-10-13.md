**Research Topic:** Fleet Management and Delivery Problems in Transportation and Logistics in India

## 1. What I Researched
- Introduction to fleet management and delivery problems in transportation and logistics.
- Major problems such as delivery delays, high operating costs, poor vehicle utilization, empty returns, and inefficient route planning.
- Root causes of these problems, including poor coordination, vehicle allocation issues, loading and unloading delays, traffic, and scheduling difficulties.
- Transportation challenges specific to liquid cargo, including water, oil, and milk.
- Tanker compatibility, cleaning requirements, cargo safety, and return-load availability.
- Transportation challenges involving perishable goods, such as fruits and vegetables, and general cargo.

## 2. What I Found
- Fleet management involves coordinating vehicles, drivers, routes, schedules, cargo, and operating costs.
- Delivery problems can arise from traffic congestion, poor planning, vehicle breakdowns, delays during loading and unloading, and communication gaps.
- Low cargo utilization and empty returns are separate problems that can increase transportation costs.
- Liquid cargo requires suitable tankers based on cargo compatibility, material, hygiene, cleaning, and safety requirements.
- A tanker designed for petroleum products cannot automatically be used for drinking water, milk, or edible oil.
- Milk and other perishable goods require timely transportation and appropriate handling to maintain quality.
- General cargo also faces challenges involving vehicle capacity, route planning, delivery schedules, and coordination.
- Research on Indian logistics and milk collection can help explain transportation inefficiencies and possible improvements.

## 3. My Thoughts
- Fleet management problems differ depending on the type of cargo being transported.
- Liquid cargo requires particular attention to tanker suitability, cleaning, safety, and return trips.
- Perishable goods need reliable schedules and careful handling to reduce quality losses.
- General cargo must also be considered to understand fleet management problems across the wider transportation sector.
- Understanding the root causes is necessary before identifying research gaps or proposing solutions.

## 4. Questions / Doubts

- How do Indian transport companies manage tanker cleaning and cargo compatibility?
