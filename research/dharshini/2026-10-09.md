Research Topic: Tomato Supply Chain and Post-Harvest Losses in Tamil Nadu
What I Researched
      • Reviewed the tomato supply chain from cultivation and harvesting to wholesale markets and retail distribution.
      • Examined causes of tomato losses, including high temperatures, rough handling, poor packaging, lack of cold storage, and transport delays.
      • Reviewed preservation approaches such as evaporative cooling, forced-air cooling, ethylene management, modified-atmosphere storage, and refrigerated transportation.
      • Considered how GPS tracking, temperature and humidity sensors, digital records, and coordination between farmers, transporters, and wholesalers could improve monitoring.
What I Found
      • Finding 1: The report identifies inadequate storage, rough handling, and inefficient transportation as major contributors to tomato wastage across the supply chain.
      • Finding 2: Evaporative cooling is presented as a relatively low-cost, farm-level option that may help preserve freshness for an additional 3–4 days, depending on conditions.
      • Finding 3: The proposed monitoring approach combines vehicle location, temperature and humidity sensors, shipment records, delay alerts, and a shared dashboard.
      • Finding 4: The report identifies a research gap in integrating loss mapping, real-time transport monitoring, affordable cooling, and collaboration among supply-chain stakeholders.
My Thoughts

      • I think tomato losses should be studied at each stage, rather than treating spoilage as a problem that happens only during transportation.
      • A possible opportunity could be a practical, low-cost system that combines simple farm-level cooling with shipment tracking and digital loss records.
      • One thing we should consider is affordability for smallholder farmers, access to electricity and water, and whether the proposed solutions can be validated through field data.
Questions / Doubts
      • How much would a basic temperature-monitoring and shipment-tracking system cost per vehicle?
      • Can evaporative cooling work effectively under the humidity and weather conditions of the selected study locations?
      • How can losses be measured consistently before loading and after delivery?
      • Would farmers, transporters, and wholesalers be willing to share shipment and demand information through one platform?
